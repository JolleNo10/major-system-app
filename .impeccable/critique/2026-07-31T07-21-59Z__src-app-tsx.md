## Design Specificity Verdict

The visual language is "dark productivity SaaS," not "mnemonic training suite." Zinc-900 cards, rounded-xl, violet accent, emoji icons could ship unchanged for any Notion-adjacent product. The app's actual subject — phonetic encoding under timing pressure — leaves almost no mark on the composition. One genuinely product-specific moment: SpeedRound's running state.
